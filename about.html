About the Department

A cleaner public-facing introduction to Maharashtra's Women and Child Development Department.

What the department does

The Women and Child Development Department was created as a separate administrative department in June 1993. Its public role covers policy, programmes, implementation and coordination for the welfare, protection, development and participation of women and children.

This redesigned page intentionally uses shorter explanations so citizens can understand where to go next instead of reading a long government-style block of text.

  • Women’s safety, welfare and empowerment
  • Child protection, development and family support
  • Nutrition and early childhood development
  • Coordination with government and partner organisations
