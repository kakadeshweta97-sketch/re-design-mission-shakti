Right to Service (RTS)

Understand the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 in plain language.

Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015

The Act has been in force since 28 April 2015. Its purpose is to make notified public services transparent, prompt and time-bound.

Time-bound service

Notified services are associated with a specified time limit and responsible officer.

Appeal route

If a notified service is delayed or rejected without proper grounds, the Act provides first and second appeal routes and a third appeal to the Commission.

Accountability

The official department information states that an erring officer may face a penalty of up to ₹5,000 per case.
