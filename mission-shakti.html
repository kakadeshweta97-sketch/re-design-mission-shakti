Mission Shakti

Safety, security and empowerment — presented as simple citizen journeys.

What is Mission Shakti?

Mission Shakti is a mission-mode framework focused on strengthening interventions for women's safety, security and empowerment. The official department describes it as a women-led development approach with support across the life cycle.

01 • Sambal

Safety and security support. The official framework includes One Stop Centres, Women Helpline, Beti Bachao Beti Padhao and Nari Adalat.

02 • Samarthya

Empowerment support, bringing together initiatives intended to improve women's access to opportunities, services and development pathways.

03 • Awareness

Public awareness, legal literacy and positive social change are presented as important parts of the mission.
